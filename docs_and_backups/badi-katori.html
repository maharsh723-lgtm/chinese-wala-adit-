The Story of the Big Bowl

In Hindi, Badi Katori translates literally to the "Big Bowl" — a vessel representing culinary abundance, familial warmth, and the unspoken joy of sharing. We honour this timeless legacy while translating it for a new generation.

Our kitchen bridges traditional street-level mastery (SRT) with progressive gourmet curation. Spices are hand-ground in-house daily; classic dishes remixed with local seasonal produce to create a texture-rich narrative in every bite.
